UMAK Medical and Dental Office Mobile Application (Reworked Version)

In 2024, we had a project where I had to design a mobile application for the university's clinic office. Its main purpose was to give students an easier way of booking an appointment with the clinic.

When I was designing it, I wanted it to be in line with modern UIs, hence the modular feel and the overstacking of "cards." During its initial creation, I thought my design was great, but seeing it again in 2026 with a different set of eyes ... it could definitely be better. So I have decided to revamp the project, and apply what I've learned from my internship experience.

For the log in, sign up, and forgot password frames, I only made simple changes. I was mostly concerned with the position and the size of the text fields, and I made the shape of the button consistent with the fields.

DASHBOARD

For the dashboard I changed how each section was presented. The previous one felt very "in your face," and while the colors worked in theory, it resulted in it looking cluttered. So I ditched the layers of color and settled for simple bold text and white rectangular text boxes.

BOOKING

Similar to the dashboard, I toned down the boxy feel and simplified the process. On the previous one, the initial booking and the rescheduling had different layouts, which I streamlined by putting the entire process in one frame.
